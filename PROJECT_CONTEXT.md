## 1. Project Overview

**Project Name:** AI-Augmented Parametric Crop Micro-Insurance Platform

**Summary:** The project modernizes agricultural risk management by replacing traditional, manual indemnity-based insurance workflows with an autonomous AI Validation Engine. It solves critical operational bottlenecks in the current landscape, specifically the 6 to 9 month delay in claim settlements caused by physical Crop Cutting Experiments (CCEs), geographic basis risk from sparse weather stations, and heavy manual backlogs involved in verifying messy, handwritten vernacular land documents.

**Target Users:** Indian smallholder and marginal farmers who typically cultivate landholdings of less than two hectares.

**Business Goal:**
- **For Insurers:** Drastic reduction in Operational Expenditure (OpEx) by eliminating physical field survey teams and manual data entry, coupled with the elimination of systemic fraud (e.g., double-insuring or inflating acreage).
- **For Farmers:** Accelerated delivery of emergency liquidity, reducing payout timelines from over 180 days to under 72 hours via automated Straight-Through Processing (STP).

**Domain Context:** The platform is built for the Indian agricultural sector, operating within the frameworks of the Pradhan Mantri Fasal Bima Yojana (PMFBY) and the Restructured Weather Based Crop Insurance Scheme (RWBCIS).

## 2. Scope

**Must-Have Features for v1 (MVP):**
- (Decided) Multimodal First Notice of Loss (FNOL) intake via a mobile-friendly frontend.
- (Decided) Intelligent Document Processing using multimodal LLMs to extract structured data from vernacular land records and handwritten policies.
- (Decided) Dynamic querying of external spatial weather APIs via an AI Agent tool-calling framework.
- (Decided) Automated rules-engine evaluation for claim adjudication (Approval, Rejection, or Manual Review).

**Nice-to-Have / Later-Phase Features:**
- (Decided) Integration of Sentinel-1 Synthetic Aperture Radar (SAR) remote sensing data to detect standing floodwater through cloud cover.
- (Suggested) Web scraping of local regional news portals and National Disaster Management Authority (NDMA) bulletins to ground-truth localized micro-climatic events (e.g., hailstorms).

**Explicitly Out of Scope:**
- (Decided) Physical field surveys and manual Crop Cutting Experiments (CCEs).
- (Decided) Subjective human damage assessment for straightforward verifiable claims.

## 3. Detailed Feature Specs

### Feature 1: Multimodal FNOL Intake
- **Purpose:** Allow farmers to register claims instantly without traveling to a physical bank or CSC.
- **User Flow:** A farmer accesses the mobile frontend, uploads a photo of their handwritten micro-insurance policy, photos of their handwritten land documents (e.g., 7/12 extract, KCC passbook), and a photograph of the flood/crop damage.
- **Inputs:** Photos/images, GPS coordinates, and timestamps.
- **Outputs:** Signed URLs generated by Firebase Storage and passed to the backend.

### Feature 2: Intelligent Document Structuring
- **Purpose:** Convert degraded vernacular documents into actionable data.
- **User Flow:** Backend receives the payload and passes it to the AI model. The Vision-Language model pre-processes the image (deskewing, binarization) and extracts fields directly from Devanagari, Gujarati, or Dravidian scripts.
- **Inputs:** Signed URLs of uploaded documents.
- **Outputs:** Structured JSON schema containing Khasra (survey plot) number, Khewat details, total holding area, insured crop species, and bank IFSC details.
- **Error Handling / Validations:** Low-confidence OCR extractions (< 0.85) are automatically routed to a Human-in-the-Loop (HITL) review queue. Numeric sequences are deterministically matched against regex patterns and checksum algorithms to prevent hallucinations.

### Feature 3: AI Validation Engine & Tool Calling
- **Purpose:** Establish the ground truth of the loss event without human surveyors.
- **User Flow:** The agent uses extracted GPS coordinates and timestamps to query external weather APIs and run asynchronous scraping workers.
- **Inputs:** Location (GPS/Pincode), event timestamp, claimed event type.
- **Outputs:** A dynamic validation and fraud score.
- **Business Rules:** The engine must triangulate data. For example, if a farmer claims flood damage, the engine queries spatial data grids and checks if local media reported a flood within a +/-24-hour window in that specific Taluka.

### Feature 4: Adjudication & Straight-Through Processing (STP)
- **Purpose:** Finalize the claim decision.
- **User Flow:** The rules engine evaluates the validation score against policy constraints.
- **Outputs:** Structured JSON payload (Approval, Rejection, or Manual Review) with step-by-step reasoning.
- **Business Rules:** If the assessed damage exceeds predefined parametric triggers (e.g., >30% crop damage corroborated by radar/news), the system executes an instant payout.

## 4. User Roles & Permissions
- **Farmer (End User):** Can upload photos/documents, submit claims, and view real-time claim status updates on the mobile interface.
- **AI Agent (System Role):** Authorized to trigger tool calls to external APIs, parse documents, run adjudication rules, and write final decisions to the database.
- **Human-in-the-Loop Reviewer (Admin):** Can view flagged claims (due to low OCR confidence or conflicting data) and manually approve or reject them.

## 5. Domain Logic & Business Rules
- **Parametric Payout Triggers (Decided):** Claims are approved if they meet deterministic conditions, such as rainfall exceeding 150mm in 24 hours (extracted from the policy) or verified crop damage exceeding 30%.
- **Fraud Cross-Checks (Decided):** The system cross-checks the declared crop species on the policy against official crop survey entries in government registries. If there is a mismatch (e.g., policy claims Soyabean, but registry shows Fallow/Maize), STP is paused.
- **Settlement Execution (Decided):** Approved claims bypass batch reviews and execute directly via the NCIP/DigiClaim module to the farmer's Aadhaar-seeded bank account using Direct Benefit Transfer (DBT) rails (UPI, IMPS, PFMS).

## 6. Data Model

**Firestore Document: Claim Record (Decided)**
- `user_id` (String, required): Link to user profile.
- `session_state` (String, required): Current status (e.g., Processing, Approved, Manual Review).
- `signed_urls` (Array of Strings, required): Links to uploaded photos.
- `final_decision` (JSON Object): Contains approval status and reasoning.

**Extraction Schema: Localized Event Corroboration (Decided)**

```json
{
  "event_type": "Hailstorm",
  "geographic_granularity": {
    "state": "Maharashtra",
    "district": "Nashik",

  },
  "event_timestamp": "2024-10-14T15:30:00Z",
  "damage_severity_score": 0.82,
  "corroboration_source_count": 4
}
```

## 7. Tech Stack & Architecture
- **Frontend (Decided):** Firebase (App Check, Storage for signed URLs, Firestore for NoSQL real-time updates). Built for mobile-friendly web access (Flutter Web).
- **Backend Orchestrator (Decided):** Cloud Run running a containerized Python FastAPI service.
- **AI / LLM Models (Decided):** Gemini 1.5 Pro via Vertex AI (for multimodal OCR, reasoning, and damage assessment).
- **Agent Framework (Decided):** Vertex AI Agent Builder / ADK for orchestrating tool calls.
- **Why these choices:** Leverages Google Cloud's serverless ecosystem to handle decoupled workloads, ensuring the frontend captures inputs quickly while the backend scales securely for complex reasoning.

## 8. APIs & Integrations
- **Weather Data APIs (Decided):** OpenWeatherMap, AgERA5 (Copernicus 0.1 deg spatial grid), CHIRPS (0.05 deg resolution), and WINDS portal feeds.
- **Government Registries (Decided):** AgriStack APIs (Farmer Registry and Crop Sowing Registry) and State Bhulekh portals (e.g., Mahabhulekh, UP Bhulekh).
- **Disbursement APIs (Decided):** NCIP / DigiClaim module.
- **Resilience Rules (Decided):** Due to API rate limits and government portal downtime, the system uses an asynchronous worker queue (Celery with Redis/RabbitMQ) with exponential backoff, jitter retries, and circuit breakers. Local static mirrors are used for fallback.

## 9. UI / UX
- **Upload Screen (Decided):** Simple UI for low-end rural smartphones to capture and upload photos of documents and damage.
- **Dashboard (Decided):** Claim status dashboard with real-time Firestore listeners to show the decision and reasoning instantly.

## 10. Non-Functional Requirements
- **Performance (Decided):** Must execute Straight-Through Processing and calculate the indemnity claim within hours, compressing the overall delivery to less than 72 hours.
- **Localization (Decided):** The AI must process inputs (documents and voice notes) in native vernacular dialects like Marathi, Hindi, Tamil, and Telugu.
- **Security (Decided):** Firebase App Check secures the application; backend runs as secure middleware via webhook triggers.

## 13. Open Questions & Risks
- **Handwriting OCR accuracy:** Degraded inputs might lead to incorrect policy term extraction. *Mitigation:* Gemini multimodal plus a confidence threshold that routes low-confidence cases to a human.
- **Prompt injection and hallucinations:** The agent might approve invalid claims. *Mitigation:* Strict structured JSON output; extraction prompts kept separate from adjudication prompts.
- **Weather API latency and reliability:** External APIs may time out. *Mitigation:* ADK tool retries and fallback caching in Cloud Run.
