# Pitch Deck Notes

Running list of things the deck must include. Add to this as we build.

## Must appear in the deck: future scope
- [ ] **WhatsApp bot** so farmers can file and track claims inside WhatsApp (requested by the team).
- [ ] **Other insurance types on the same engine**: livestock, health, motor, property, weather-indexed MSME cover. Each product plugs in its own documents, evidence checks, rules and payout formula (requested by the team).
- [ ] Policy enrollment and purchase flow (the POC covers claims only).
- [ ] More Indian languages: Marathi, Tamil, Telugu, Bengali, Gujarati.
- [ ] Real integrations: AgriStack, Bhulekh land records, NCIP/DigiClaim, WINDS weather stations.
- [ ] Real payouts over DBT rails (UPI, IMPS, PFMS).
- [ ] SMS and IVR access for feature-phone farmers.

## Map slides to the judging weights
- Technical merit and GenAI use (40%): multimodal document reading, an evidence-gathering agent, Gemini with Google Search grounding, Sentinel-1 radar, the truth score.
- Problem alignment and impact (25%): PMFBY payouts take 6–9 months versus our target of under 72 hours, plus fraud reduction.
- Innovation (25%): AI checks the claim against several independent sources, and every decision is explained in the farmer's language.
- UX (10%): voice in Hindi, guided filing, one-click approval for reviewers.
